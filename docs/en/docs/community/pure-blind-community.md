# Pure Blind Community Guide

**Pure Blind** is Fuxi Legion's main production region. This page introduces the local map, home station setup, recommended income options for new members, voice channels, and corporation buyback and contact channels.

Detailed instructions for each activity are linked in the guide index at the end of the page.

> **Data as of October 2026.** Income figures marked `xxM` are placeholders in the source document. Actual income depends on the game version and market. Check current corporation announcements for updated figures.

## Set your home station

1. Enter `5ZXX-K - Uncle Huang's retire house (Fuxi Holding)` in the in-game search field and set it as your destination.
2. Dock at the structure. Click your portrait in the upper-left corner, open **Home Station**, choose **Set Home Station**, and select the current station.

![Find the staging structure in search](/images/pure-blind-community/02-clone-rally-01.webp)

## Member activities and priorities

- **Dreadnought and carrier beacon ratting:** Requires five characters. You can run beacons solo with multiple accounts or group up with other pilots. Estimated income is `xxM` ISK per tick (20 minutes) per character.
- **Smartbomb ratting:** Form a group to smartbomb Haven anomalies in the yellow-circle systems. Estimated income is `xxM`.
- **Vexor ratting at Rally Points:** Earn enough ISK to cover a Vexor loss. Most of the income comes from 6/10 DED escalations, worth about 130M ISK each.
- **Ishtar ratting at Small Havens:** Estimated bounty income is `xxM` ISK, plus `xxM` from escalations.
- **Mordu's Legion missions:** Earn Loyalty Points (LP); requires three characters. Hourly income in ISK depends on the current LP conversion value and is marked `xxM`.
- **Sisters of EVE missions:** Earn LP and require multiple characters. Hourly income in ISK depends on the current LP conversion value and is marked `xxM`.

### Suggested income activities for new players who are not spending real money

**When a standing fleet is available** (usually after daily downtime, from around 19:00 to 22:30 in the UTC+8 time zone):

- **Apocalypse smartbomb ratting:** Group up in a yellow-circle system.
- **Capital beacon ratting:** Group up with Dominix battleships in a blue-circle system.

**When no standing fleet is available** (for example, if you cannot join voice comms):

- **Vexor ratting at Rally Points:** The corporation provides replaceable Vexors and buys the escalations at a fixed price.
- **Scan signatures and harvest gas:** The corporation provides mining ships and a buyback program.
- **Ishtar ratting at Small Havens:** The corporation buys escalations at a fixed price.

### Activities for spare time

- Relic and data site exploration
- Gas and ore mining
