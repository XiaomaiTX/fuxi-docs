# Alliance Services

Fuxi Legion is a member corporation of **Winter Coalition (WC)**. Founded in October 2017, Winter Coalition grew out of the OSY Chinese-speaking community and is led by Fraternity. It brings together alliances across time zones and language groups. Its community is divided mainly between Chinese-speaking WC-CN and English-speaking WC-EN.

This page summarizes alliance services and links to their documentation. Rates, locations, and rules can change, so check the **alliance Wiki and announcements** before using a service.

:::tip Services can change

This page is an overview and directory. For current prices, locations, collateral requirements, and other details, refer to the relevant pages in the [Winter Coalition Wiki](https://wiki.winterco.org/zh/start).

:::

## Accounts and identity: SeAT

[SeAT](https://wiki.winterco.org/zh/guide/it/seat) (<https://seat.winterco.org>) is the alliance's identity, data, and service management platform. You need it to access other alliance services.

- After registering with your EVE Online account, link **all characters** on your account.
- SeAT synchronizes access to alliance voice comms, Discord, and other services.
- Use SeAT to check participation credit (PAP) and submit ship replacement (SRP) requests.
- Characters linked together in SeAT are treated as **one member**. Member requirements are counted per member, not per character.

## Communications and fleet coordination

### Voice comms: Mumble

[Mumble](https://wiki.winterco.org/zh/guide/it/mumble) is the alliance's voice communications platform. Alliance members are required to **join voice comms while logged into the game** so that fleets and rescue operations can be organized quickly.

| Item        | Details                                                                                        |
| ----------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Client      | <https://www.mumble.info/downloads/>                                                           |
| Requirement | Join Fuxi Legion and link your account in SeAT.                                                |
| Setup       | Configure push-to-talk ("blue mic"); setting up local mute ("yellow mic") is also recommended. |

:::warning Beware of fake websites

`mumble.com` is not affiliated with the official Mumble project. Download Mumble only from <https://www.mumble.info/downloads/>.

:::

The alliance runs voice servers in several regions, including mainland China, Europe, Singapore, Australia, North America, and South Africa. See the [Mumble guide](https://wiki.winterco.org/zh/guide/it/mumble) for connection details.

### Messages and fleet pings: Discord and QQ

[Discord](https://wiki.winterco.org/zh/guide/it/discord) is the alliance's main text and voice community. [QQ](https://wiki.winterco.org/zh/guide/it/qq) is used for rally notices to members in China.

Common Discord channel categories include:

| Category              | Example channels                                                  | Purpose                                                                  |
| --------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Emergencies and intel | `#help-my-rorq-tackled`, `#attack-notifications`                  | Request help when a Rorqual is tackled; receive structure-attack alerts. |
| Military              | `#frt-alliance-pings`, `#frt-military-pings`, `#frt-mining-pings` | Alliance and military announcements, moon mining, and mining fleets.     |
| Community             | `#general`, `#pvp`, `#trading`, `#services`                       | General chat, PvP discussion, trading, and service listings.             |
| IT support            | `#helpdesk`, `#shipping-helpdesk`                                 | Technical support and courier questions.                                 |

The alliance also has German, Spanish, French, Russian, and other language channels. Link your Discord account in SeAT for full access.

## Logistics, industry, and trade

### Courier and freight services

The alliance provides small-parcel courier and bulk freight services. A key service is [S.F Express](https://wiki.winterco.org/zh/services/courier/%E9%A1%BA%E4%B8%B0%E5%BF%AB%E9%80%92).

- Create an in-game **private courier contract** and assign it to the courier service.
- **Always enter collateral.** Collateral is the only source of compensation if a shipment is lost; contracts without it will be rejected.
- Do not include plastic wraps in a contract; contracts containing packaged containers cannot be returned.
- Fees are based on volume (ISK/m³) or the distance for a dedicated ship. Minimum charges and volume limits apply. See the [courier services overview](https://wiki.winterco.org/zh/services/courier/start).
- S.F Express provides a shipping calculator: <https://sf-express.winterco.org/>.

### Buyback

[Panda Logistics](https://wiki.winterco.org/zh/services/buyback/panda_logistics) is the alliance's non-profit buyback service, intended to help members convert assets into ISK and support alliance logistics.

- It buys minerals, salvage, derived materials, Planetary Industry products, and other eligible items. Blueprints, damaged items, assembled ships, Abyssal items, and some other goods are excluded.
- The service buys at approximately 95% of Jita buy orders; check the alliance Wiki for current terms. Submit quotes through <https://www.e-351.com/buyback/panda-logistics>.
- Add a **Janice static appraisal link** to the contract description to lock in the appraisal. Contracts without it may be rejected if market prices change.

### Capital manufacturing and industry

The alliance operates capital and supercapital manufacturing structures in several regions. Its industrial map shows the locations and rules for industry hubs, reaction hubs, and Planetary Industry areas. See:

- [Alliance capital manufacturing and industry guide](https://wiki.winterco.org/zh/services/capital_manufacture/start).
- [Alliance services handbook, including industrial structure locations](https://wiki.winterco.org/zh/guide/corporation_handbook/setcr_handbook/%E8%81%94%E7%9B%9F/%E8%81%94%E7%9B%9F%E6%9C%8D%E5%8A%A1).

### Market and trading rules

The alliance has rules for internal markets and contracts. These include restrictions on reselling items bought through alliance contracts or markets at a markup, and on selling supercapital ships to neutral or hostile groups. See the [market rules](https://wiki.winterco.org/zh/rules/trade/markets).

## Community, governance, and diplomacy

- **Alliance rules:** Members are subject to the [alliance rules](https://wiki.winterco.org/zh/rules/start), whose core principle is "be friendly and honest; stand together and help one another." Report disputes to your corporation leadership first; they will contact alliance leadership if needed.
- **Diplomacy:** The [alliance diplomats](https://wiki.winterco.org/zh/coalition/leadership) handle disputes with allies and external groups. The alliance also publishes its current Non-Invasion Pact (NIP) agreements.
- **Alliance forum:** <https://forums.winterco.org/> for discussion, proposals, and official announcements.
- **Alliance updates:** [Alliance News and Updates](https://wiki.winterco.org/zh/alliance_news_and_update/start) and the [Daily of Alliance log](https://wiki.winterco.org/zh/daily_of_alliance/start).
- **Tutorial resources:** The [tutorial section](https://wiki.winterco.org/zh/guide/start) collects alliance guides, and [Overview settings](https://wiki.winterco.org/zh/guide/overviews) provides recommended overview settings.

## Rental services

The alliance offers [rental space](https://wiki.winterco.org/zh/renting). Tenants pay regular fees to use selected unused systems for industry. The rental alliance, Azure Citizen, manages the service, which is primarily aimed at players focused on production.
