# Corporation Services

As a long-established large corporation in EVE Online, Fuxi Legion provides a range of member services and activities.

## Corporation IT platform

[Fuxi SeAT](https://seat.fuxi-legion.com) is the corporation's central operations platform. It supports **fleet loss reimbursement**, **benefit distribution**, **skill planning**, and **character management**.

## Ferrari Express courier service

Ferrari Express is an industrial hauling corporation affiliated with Fuxi Legion. It provides convenient courier services to members in the corporation's area of operations.

For current rates and service details, check the **rally group announcements** or the [official website](https://ferrari-express.nantianmen.space). Follow whichever source has the latest information.

## Dedicated industrial system

**V0DF-2** in the Tribute region is a **dedicated industrial system** for Fuxi Legion and its affiliated industrial corporations, with related industry services available.
