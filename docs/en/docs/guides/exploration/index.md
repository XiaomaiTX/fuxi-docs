# Exploration and Events

This section collects guides to exploration and in-game events. It focuses on scanning cosmic signatures (often called "relic running" in the Chinese community), seasonal event guides and archives, and missions and stories related to EVE lore.

## Topics

- **Cosmic signatures:** Scan and hack relic and data sites for blueprints, materials, and rare items.
- **Wormholes:** Scanning, survival, and wormhole mechanics.
- **Special missions:** EVE lore, agents, and epic arcs.
- **Seasonal events:** Recurring events such as Winter Nexus and Crimson Harvest, with guides and archives for each release.
