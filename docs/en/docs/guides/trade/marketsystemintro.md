# Market Systems

Players have established a handful of systems as the main trade hubs in EVE Online, with trading concentrated at particular stations and structures. **Jita** is the price benchmark for New Eden; prices for most non-capital items are compared against Jita.

In addition to Jita, trade takes place in markets across high-sec, low-sec, and null-sec. The sections below introduce the main hubs.

## High-sec markets

High-sec markets have developed around the four empires. Following the 2026 New Player Experience refresh and the addition of the Genesis starting area, Genesis also has an official market area.

:::warning Watch out for war targets and suicide ganks

CONCORD NPCs enforce the law in high-sec, and players cannot attack others freely. However, they can still destroy your ship in either of these situations:

1. Your corporation or alliance is at war with the attacker. The attacker can legally destroy your ship, and CONCORD will not respond. Characters in **Fraternity. (FRT.)** should avoid high-sec where possible.
2. You are flying a fragile ship with valuable cargo or fittings, and an attacker chooses to destroy it to loot the wreck. This is called a **suicide gank**. See [How to Avoid High-Sec Ganks](/en/docs/guides/trade/howtoavoidhighsecgank/) for ways to reduce the risk.

:::

### Caldari high-sec markets

**Jita:** New Eden's main market hub. **Jita IV - Moon 4 - Caldari Navy Assembly Plant** is the largest NPC trade station in the game. You can buy or sell nearly any non-capital hull or item here through the market or contracts. Jita is the baseline for prices across New Eden.

**Perimeter:** One gate from Jita. Often called the "Quiet Trade Hub," it has many player-owned structures where trading fees may be lower than at NPC stations.

## Low-sec markets

Low-sec is dangerous, but it has active markets. Capital ships cannot enter high-sec, so capital trading usually takes place in low-sec or null-sec. Alliance and corporation deployments have also created markets in low-sec focused on supplying fleets and staging areas.

### Amarr and Minmatar Factional Warfare space

Amamake has a publicly accessible Keepstar operated as a neutral hub. It sells some ships and fittings used in Factional Warfare. With less competition than Jita, prices are often more than twice as high.

Todifrauan is a major gathering point for the Factional Warfare community and one of the alliance's routes into low-sec. A Fortizar there has market services, mainly for Factional Warfare ships, fittings, and ammunition. The source document's note on prices is incomplete.

### Low-sec asset-safety markets

When a player-owned null-sec structure is destroyed, assets that were not moved are delivered by asset safety to designated stations in low-sec. These delivery systems have formed their own markets, often including supercapital hulls and player assets.

## Null-sec markets

Null-sec markets are largely organized by major alliances. Moving goods between null-sec and high-sec depends on player hauling services. Sovereignty-based industry and the ability to build supercapital ships also contribute to a distinct market system.
