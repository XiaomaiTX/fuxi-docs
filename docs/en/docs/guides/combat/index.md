# Combat and Fleets

This section collects guides to combat and fleet operations, corresponding to the official **Enforcer** and **Soldier of Fortune** career paths.

## Topics

- **PvE combat:** Combat anomalies, DED complexes, missions, and NPC faction engagements.
- **PvP:** Solo engagements, small-gang ambushes, and anti-ganking.
- **Fleet operations:** Fleet Commanders (FCs), doctrine fleets, participation credit (PAP), and ship replacement (SRP).
- **Ship fittings:** Fitting principles and common fleet doctrines.
