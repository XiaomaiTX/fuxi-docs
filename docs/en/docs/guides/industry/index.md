# Industry and Manufacturing

This section collects guides to Planetary Industry, mining, industry, and manufacturing, starting with **Planetary Industry (PI)**, which can generate ISK while you are offline. It offers a look at EVE Online's extensive industry system and the ways to play beyond flying spaceships.

## Topics

- **Mining:** Mining ore, ice, moon ore, and gas, with comparisons of methods and income.
- **Reprocessing and reactions:** Ore reprocessing, moon reactions, and material chains.
- **Manufacturing:** Blueprint research, material efficiency, and common ship production workflows.
- **Planetary Industry:** Setting up production on planets and planning output (colloquially called "farming" in Chinese).
- **Industrial structures:** Using corporation and alliance industry structures.
