# Fuxi Legion Omnipotence Temple

_Fuxi is one of the more ~serious~ Chinese-speaking corporations on Tranquility. It has regularly placed among the top ten EVE corporations for PvP on [zKillboard](https://zkillboard.com/corporation/98185110/ranks/) and among the top five Chinese corporations in the [Ningjing Weekly PvP ranking](https://space.bilibili.com/1273724812). In 2021, Fuxi won first place in CCP's [corporation recruitment video contest](https://www.eveonline.com/news/view/corp-propaganda-contest-the-winners), earning the right to display its video in NPC stations._

_The true celestial host, Fuxi, is blessed by the universe's five primordial gods. HY, the Golden King of Avalon and supreme black-robed archmage of New Eden, leads 161 mechanical warlords, psychic sages, and tactical masters, along with 2,136 immortal super-soldiers, to sweep through dozens of leading foreign PvP corporations and contest New Eden's northwestern frontier. Wherever the golden fleet goes, the heavens darken, the sun and moon fade, dimensions shake, and reality itself cracks. Countless beings feel its power in every cell and bow in awe._

_(Omnipotence comes from the Latin *omnipotens*, meaning all-powerful.)_
